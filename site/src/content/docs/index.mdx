## Install once, run it from anywhere

The pgbx extension lives inside Postgres and does the backups. The pgbx CLI is the client: you, or your AI agent through the pgbx skill, use it from a laptop or CI, straight or over SSH.

<HowItWorks />

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Nothing to remember" icon="rocket">
    No cron jobs, no backup scripts. Databases you create later are picked up on their own and
    backed up within a minute. Each one is kept for 14 days by default.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Any S3 storage" icon="seti:folder">
    AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, Hetzner, Backblaze B2, MinIO: anything that speaks S3. Your backups sit
    in your bucket, under your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Getting data back" icon="random">
    `pgbx db-restore` brings a database back as a new copy, so you can check it before switching.
    Server gone? It restores straight from your bucket onto a fresh one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tells you what's wrong" icon="setting">
    `pgbx status` shows every database and its last backup. `pgbx doctor` explains a failed backup,
    or why Postgres won't start, in plain words.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
